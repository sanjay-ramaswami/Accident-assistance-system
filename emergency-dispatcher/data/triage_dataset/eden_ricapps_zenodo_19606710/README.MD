# Clinical‑functional classification of emergency department presenting complaints
## Based on the Manchester Triage System (EDEN‑RICAPPS)

---

## Overview

This repository contains a **clinical‑functional classification of emergency
department presenting complaints**, inspired by the Manchester Triage System and
developed as a **methodological output of the EDEN‑RICAPPS project**.

The purpose of this classification is to support **early risk stratification**
and methodological research in emergency medicine using information available at
the time of initial emergency department presentation.

The materials provided here are intended for **research and methodological use**
and do not constitute a clinical decision‑making tool.

---

## Relationship to the EDEN‑RICAPPS project

EDEN‑RICAPPS is a collaborative clinical research project led by SEMES (Spanish
Society of Emergency Medicine) and RICAPPS (Research Network on Chronicity, Primary
Care and Health Promotion), focused on identifying patients aged **65 years and
older** at increased risk of early adverse outcomes after emergency department
attendance.

This dataset represents a **derived methodological product** of the EDEN‑RICAPPS
project. The **original study protocol**, including objectives, design, eligibility
criteria and outcomes, is **registered and publicly available on ClinicalTrials**
(NCT07194122).

This Zenodo record does **not** include patient‑level data, results, or the study
protocol.

## Contents of this dataset

The repository includes the following files:

- **`classification_manchester_ed_presenting_complaints.xlsx`**  
  Main dataset providing the mapping between diagnostic or presenting‑complaint
  codes and clinical‑functional chapters.

- **`data_dictionary_manchester_classification.md`**  
  Detailed description of dataset variables and allowed values.

- **`classification_rules.md`**  
  Conceptual and clinical rules underlying the classification.

These materials together constitute the full specification of the classification.

---

## Clinical‑functional chapters

The classification defines **eight mutually exclusive clinical‑functional
chapters**:

1. Cardiorespiratory  
2. Acute neurological  
3. Sepsis / potential infection  
4. Medical–surgical abdominal  
5. Major trauma  
6. Debility and fatigue  
7. Initial low vital risk  
8. Other complaints  

Each diagnostic or presenting‑complaint code must be assigned to **one and only
one chapter**.

---

## Scope and limitations

- This classification is **clinical‑functional**, not diagnostic.
- It reflects **initial functional risk**, not final diagnosis or outcome.
- It is **not a triage algorithm** and should not be used for bedside clinical
  decision‑making.
- Distribution of chapters may vary across institutions due to local coding
  practices.

The category *“Debility and fatigue”* is defined in the framework even if it may
be empty in some cohorts, reflecting common clinical coding approaches in which
functional deterioration is attributed to specific etiologies.

---

## Intended use

This dataset is intended for:

- observational research in emergency medicine,
- early risk stratification studies,
- methodological development,
- epidemiological analyses,
- quality and safety research.
